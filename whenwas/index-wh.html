🌍 How to play WhenWasTimeLine?

👆

Order the cards: Drag and drop historical milestones from oldest (top) to newest (bottom).

🔍

Check order: Press check. Correct ones turn green, close ones yellow, errors red.

❤️

Limited lives: You have 5 overall attempts to sort the entire timeline correctly.
